🧠 Why Aegis AI Needs Hindsight Memory
Standard conversational AI systems suffer from amnesia—treating every interaction as day zero. In healthcare, forgetting previous context is clinically dangerous and frustrating. Hindsight provides a persistent memory architecture based on Retain, Recall, and Reflect:
1. Previous Interactions
Tracks total visits, admission frequency, and long-term patient relationship.
2. Patient Preferences
Remembers preferred doctors (Dr. Smith), hospitals, and appointment slots.
3. Conversation History
Recalls multi-day symptom reports and notices worsening conditions.
4. Past Information for Safety
Cross-checks drug allergies (e.g. Penicillin) to prevent fatal contraindications.
